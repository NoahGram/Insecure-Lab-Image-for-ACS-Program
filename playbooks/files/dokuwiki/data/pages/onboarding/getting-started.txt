====== Onboarding: Getting Started ======

Welcome to MegaBit Security. This short checklist helps new trainees get started in the environment.

**Steps**

  * Confirm access to the training VM
  * Read the internal wiki pages under the `company` namespace
  * Complete the basic lab: "Intro to the network"
  * If you encounter issues, open a ticket with `it-team@MegaBit.local`

Good luck and have fun learning!
