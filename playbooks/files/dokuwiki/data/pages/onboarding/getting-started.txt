====== Onboarding: Getting Started at LabSys ======

Welcome to **LabSys Inc.**! We're excited to have you join our team. This guide will help you get set up and productive in your first weeks.

----

===== Day 1 Checklist =====

  * [ ] Pick up your laptop and badge from IT (Building A, Room 102)
  * [ ] Complete new hire paperwork with HR
  * [ ] Set up your email account (Roundcube Webmail)
  * [ ] Enroll in Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
  * [ ] Review the :it:security policy

----

===== Week 1 Checklist =====

  * [ ] Complete mandatory security awareness training (HR portal)
  * [ ] Read the :company:about page to learn about LabSys
  * [ ] Review :company:hr:policies for company guidelines
  * [ ] Set up VPN access for remote work
  * [ ] Meet with your manager to discuss goals and expectations
  * [ ] Join relevant Slack channels for your team

----

===== For Engineering Roles =====

If you're joining the engineering team, also complete these steps:

  * [ ] Request access to the engineering VLAN (submit ticket to IT)
  * [ ] Install LabSys IDE and ChipSim from :engineering:software-tools
  * [ ] Review the :engineering:products page for current product lines
  * [ ] Get access to the Git repositories (BitBucket/GitHub Enterprise)
  * [ ] Complete export control training if working on ITAR/EAR projects

===== Company Culture =====

At LabSys, we value:
  * **Innovation** — Push boundaries in chip design and technology
  * **Collaboration** — Work together across teams and geographies
  * **Integrity** — Protect our IP and maintain ethical standards
  * **Excellence** — Deliver quality in everything we do

----

===== Tips for Success =====

  * Don't hesitate to ask questions — everyone was new once!
  * Use Slack for quick questions, email for formal communication
  * Document your work in the wiki to help others
  * Attend the monthly all-hands meeting (first Friday of each month)

----

**Welcome aboard! We're glad to have you at LabSys.**
