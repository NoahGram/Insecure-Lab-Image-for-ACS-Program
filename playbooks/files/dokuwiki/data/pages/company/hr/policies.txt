====== HR: Policies & Guidelines ======

This page provides an overview of LabSys Inc. human resources policies. All employees are expected to read and comply with these guidelines.

----

===== Work Hours & Attendance =====

| **Standard Hours**    | 09:00 - 17:30 (flexible start between 08:00-10:00) |
| **Core Hours**        | 10:00 - 15:00 (required presence)                  |
| **Lunch Break**       | 1 hour (typically 12:00 - 13:00)                   |
| **Remote Work**       | Up to 2 days/week with manager approval            |

----

===== Leave & Time Off =====

  * **Annual Leave**: 20 days per year (prorated for new hires)
  * **Sick Leave**: 10 days per year
  * **Parental Leave**: 12 weeks paid (birth/adoption)
  * **Bereavement**: 5 days for immediate family

To request leave, submit through the HR portal or email hr@labsys.com at least 2 weeks in advance for planned absences.

----

===== Acceptable Use Policy =====

  * Company equipment is for business-related activities only
  * Personal use of company devices should be minimal and not interfere with work
  * Installation of unauthorized software is prohibited
  * All work product created using company resources is property of LabSys Inc.

----

===== Code of Conduct =====

  * Treat all colleagues with respect and professionalism
  * Maintain confidentiality of proprietary chip designs and trade secrets
  * Report any conflicts of interest to your manager or HR
  * Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated

----

===== Expense Reimbursement =====

  * Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense
  * Receipts required for expenses over $25
  * Pre-approval needed for travel expenses exceeding $500
  * Reimbursement processed within 10 business days

----

===== Performance Reviews =====

  * Annual reviews conducted in December
  * Mid-year check-ins in June
  * Goal setting aligned with departmental objectives

----

===== Confidentiality & IP =====

All employees must sign and adhere to:
  * Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
  * Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement
  * Export Control Compliance (ITAR/EAR where applicable)

**Reminder**: LabSys chip designs, fabrication processes, and customer data are strictly confidential.
