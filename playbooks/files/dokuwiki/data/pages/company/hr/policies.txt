====== HR: Policies ======

This page summarizes a small set of HR policies. These are intentionally simplified for exercises.

  * Work hours: 09:00 - 17:30
  * Leave requests: submit to HR (hr@MegaBit.local)
  * Acceptable use: Company machines are for work-related tasks and approved training only

Security-sensitive policies (passwords, credentials) are not stored here — use the secret management exercises instead.
