====== About LabSys Inc. ======

**LabSys Inc.** is a semiconductor company founded in 2012, headquartered in San Jose, California. We design and manufacture high-performance chips for IoT, artificial intelligence, and embedded systems markets.

----

===== Our Mission =====

To deliver innovative, energy-efficient semiconductor solutions that power the next generation of intelligent devices and systems.

===== Our Vision =====

To be the global leader in specialized chip solutions for edge computing and AI applications.
