====== Engineering: Product Portfolio ======

LabSys Inc. specializes in high-performance semiconductor solutions for IoT, artificial intelligence, and embedded systems. This page provides an overview of our current product lines.

----

===== LS-1000 Series — Low-Power Microcontrollers =====

The LS-1000 series is designed for battery-powered IoT devices requiring ultra-low power consumption with reliable performance.

| **Model**      | **Core**         | **Clock Speed** | **RAM**   | **Flash** | **Power (Active)** |
| LS-1010        | ARM Cortex-M0+   | 48 MHz          | 32 KB     | 256 KB    | 45 µA/MHz          |
| LS-1020        | ARM Cortex-M4    | 80 MHz          | 64 KB     | 512 KB    | 55 µA/MHz          |
| LS-1030        | ARM Cortex-M4F   | 120 MHz         | 128 KB    | 1 MB      | 60 µA/MHz          |

**Key Features**:
  * Ultra-low power modes (down to 0.5 µA in deep sleep)
  * Integrated BLE 5.0 radio (LS-1020/1030)
  * Hardware security module (AES-256, SHA-256)
  * Temperature range: -40°C to +85°C

**Target Applications**: Smart sensors, wearables, asset trackers, smart home devices

----

===== LS-2000 Series — AI Accelerators =====

The LS-2000 series delivers high-performance neural network acceleration for edge AI applications.

| **Model**      | **NPU Cores** | **TOPS** | **Memory Interface** | **TDP**   |
| LS-2010        | 4             | 2        | LPDDR4, 32-bit       | 3W        |
| LS-2020        | 8             | 8        | LPDDR4X, 64-bit      | 8W        |
| LS-2050        | 16            | 20       | LPDDR5, 128-bit      | 15W       |

**Key Features**:
  * Support for TensorFlow Lite, ONNX, PyTorch models
  * INT8/INT16 quantization for optimized inference
  * Integrated ISP for camera input (up to 4K @ 30fps)
  * PCIe Gen4 interface for host connectivity

**Target Applications**: Security cameras, autonomous robots, industrial inspection, medical imaging

----

===== LS-3000 Series — Embedded Memory =====

The LS-3000 series provides high-density, low-latency memory solutions optimized for embedded systems.

| **Model**      | **Type**      | **Capacity** | **Speed**       | **Interface**    |
| LS-3010        | SRAM          | 4 MB         | 200 MHz         | Parallel         |
| LS-3020        | PSRAM         | 16 MB        | 166 MHz QPI     | Quad SPI         |
| LS-3050        | HBM2e         | 2 GB         | 3.2 Gbps        | 1024-bit         |

**Key Features**:
  * Low-power operation (< 50 mW active for LS-3010)
  * ECC support for mission-critical applications
  * Wide temperature range (-40°C to +105°C)
  * Automotive-grade options available (AEC-Q100)

**Target Applications**: Automotive ADAS, industrial controllers, networking equipment
